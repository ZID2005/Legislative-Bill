THE TELANGANA MOTOR VEHICLES TAXATION (AMENDMENT) BILL, 2024
L.A. Bill No. 8 of 2024
A Bill further to amend the Telangana Motor Vehicles Taxation Act, 1963.

Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Telangana as follows:-
1. Short title.- This Act may be called the Telangana Motor Vehicles Taxation (Amendment) Act, 2024.
2. Revision of life-time tax on private vehicles and introduction of concession rates for Electric Vehicles (EVs).
3. Imposition of green tax on commercial vehicles aged over fifteen years to curb vehicular pollution.

STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS
To promote clean energy mobility, incentivize the adoption of electric vehicles across Telangana, and disincentivize aging polluting vehicles through targeted tax rationalization under the Transport Department.
