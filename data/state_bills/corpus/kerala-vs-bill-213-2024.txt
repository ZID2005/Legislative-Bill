THE NON-RESIDENT KERALITES' WELFARE (AMENDMENT) BILL, 2024
പ്രവാസി കേരളീയ ക്ഷേമ (ഭേദഗതി) ബിൽ, 2024
Bill No. 213 of 2024
A Bill further to amend the Non-Resident Keralites' Welfare Act, 2008.

Be it enacted by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Kerala as follows:-
1. Short title and commencement.- (1) This Act may be called the Non-Resident Keralites' Welfare (Amendment) Act, 2024.
2. Amendment of Section 15.- Enhancement of monthly pension contributions and survivor benefits for registered Non-Resident Keralites.
3. Extension of welfare board benefits to returning migrant workers and gig employees abroad.

STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS
Millions of Keralites work overseas and in other Indian States, contributing significantly to the State economy. This legislation strengthens social security, medical assistance, and rehabilitation funds managed by the Non-Resident Keralites Affairs (NORKA) Department.
