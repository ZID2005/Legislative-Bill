THE TELANGANA PUBLIC HEALTH AND EPIDEMICS (AMENDMENT) BILL, 2023
L.A. Bill No. 9 of 2023
A Bill to amend the Telangana Public Health Act, 1939.

Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Telangana as follows:-
1. Short title.- This Act may be called the Telangana Public Health and Epidemics (Amendment) Act, 2023.
2. Enhanced statutory powers for District Medical Officers to declare epidemic containment zones and enforce sanitation directives.
3. Mandatory reporting of communicable diseases by private medical diagnostic centers within twelve hours.

STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS
Post-pandemic healthcare governance requires robust disease surveillance, rapid epidemic response mechanisms, and real-time public health reporting across urban and rural Telangana.
