THE PREVENTION OF CRUELTY TO ANIMALS (KERALA AMENDMENT) BILL, 2025
Bill No. 271 of 2025
A Bill to amend the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals Act, 1960 in its application to the State of Kerala.

Be it enacted by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Kerala as follows:-
1. Short title and extent.- (1) This Act may be called the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals (Kerala Amendment) Act, 2025.
(2) It extends to the whole of the State of Kerala.
2. Mandatory registration of commercial breeding centers, pet shops, and veterinary shelter houses.
3. Enhanced penalties and fines for abandonment and cruelty against domestic and stray animals.
4. Establishment of District Animal Welfare Oversight Committees headed by District Magistrates.

STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS
In recent years, humane treatment of domestic and community animals has gained critical public importance. This amendment establishes stringent regulatory oversight over commercial breeding, pet boarding facilities, and humane population control under the Animal Husbandry Department.
