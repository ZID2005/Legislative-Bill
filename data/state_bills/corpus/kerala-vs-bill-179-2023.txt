THE KERALA VETERINARY AND ANIMAL SCIENCES UNIVERSITY (AMENDMENT) BILL, 2023
Bill No. 179 of 2023
A Bill to amend the Kerala Veterinary and Animal Sciences University Act, 2010.

Be it enacted by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Kerala as follows:-
1. Short title.- This Act may be called the Kerala Veterinary and Animal Sciences University (Amendment) Act, 2023.
2. Reconstitution of the University Board of Management and Academic Council.
3. Promotion of livestock clinical research, dairy technology incubators, and student welfare standards.

STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS
To align the governance of the Veterinary University with contemporary higher education standards and strengthen veterinary diagnostics and research capacity.
