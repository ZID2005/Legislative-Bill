THE KERALA REPEALING AND SAVING BILL, 2024
Bill No. 220 of 2024
A Bill to repeal certain obsolete and redundant enactments in the State of Kerala.

Be it enacted by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Kerala as follows:-
1. Short title.- This Act may be called the Kerala Repealing and Saving Act, 2024.
2. Repeal of obsolete enactments.- The enactments specified in the First Schedule are hereby repealed to the extent mentioned therein.
3. Savings.- The repeal by this Act of any enactment shall not affect any other enactment in which the repealed enactment has been applied, incorporated or referred to.

STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS
The Law Reforms Commission undertook a comprehensive review of state legislation and identified archaic and spent enactments. This bill removes obsolete statutes from the statute book, simplifying legal governance and improving clarity.
