THE TELANGANA PANCHAYAT RAJ (AMENDMENT) BILL, 2024
తెలంగాణ పంచాయతీ రాజ్ (సవరణ) బిల్లు, 2024
L.A. Bill No. 3 of 2024
A Bill to amend the Telangana Panchayat Raj Act, 2018.

Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Telangana as follows:-
1. Short title.- This Act may be called the Telangana Panchayat Raj (Amendment) Act, 2024.
2. Amendment of Section 15.- Tenure and administrative powers of Gram Panchayat special officers during transitional terms.
3. Financial devolution of state grants directly to Village Panchayat accounts for drinking water and sanitization works.

STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS
To ensure continuity of rural governance, uninterrupted delivery of civic services, and transparent financial grant transfers to local Gram Panchayats.
