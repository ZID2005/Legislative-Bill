THE KERALA PANCHAYAT RAJ (SECOND AMENDMENT) BILL, 2024
കേരള പഞ്ചായത്ത് രാജ് (രണ്ടാം ഭേദഗതി) ബിൽ, 2024
Bill No. 196 of 2024
A Bill further to amend the Kerala Panchayat Raj Act, 1994.

Be it enacted by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Kerala as follows:-
1. Short title and commencement.- (1) This Act may be called the Kerala Panchayat Raj (Second Amendment) Act, 2024.
(2) It shall come into force at once.
2. Amendment of Section 6.- In the Kerala Panchayat Raj Act, 1994 (13 of 1994), in section 6, provisions regarding ward delimitation based on latest population data are updated.
3. Civic power devolution and electronic service delivery mandates across Village Panchayats.

STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS
Rapid urbanization in rural fringe areas requires rationalization of Gram Panchayat wards and streamlined digital service delivery. This bill empowers Local Self-Government institutions to modernize civic administration and ward representation.
