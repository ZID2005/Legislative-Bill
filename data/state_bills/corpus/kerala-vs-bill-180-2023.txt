THE KERALA BOVINE BREEDING BILL, 2023
Bill No. 180 of 2023
A Bill to regulate bovine breeding activities, production and sale of bovine semen, and artificial insemination services in Kerala.

Be it enacted by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Kerala as follows:-
1. Short title and commencement.- (1) This Act may be called the Kerala Bovine Breeding Act, 2023.
2. Establishment of the Kerala Bovine Breeding Regulatory Authority.
3. Certification standards for bulls, semen stations, and artificial insemination technicians.
4. Penalties for unauthorized sale of unregistered semen doses.

STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS
To safeguard the genetic quality of dairy cattle in Kerala, improve milk yields, and protect livestock owners against substandard breeding inputs through certification and quality control.
