THE KERALA PUBLIC RECORDS BILL, 2023
Bill No. 174 of 2023
A Bill to regulate the management, administration and preservation of public records of the State Government, local authorities, and public sector undertakings.

Be it enacted by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Kerala as follows:-
1. Short title and extent.- (1) This Act may be called the Kerala Public Records Act, 2023.
2. Appointment of Records Officers in all departments and statutory bodies.
3. Prohibition of unauthorized destruction, de-accessioning, or export of public archives.
4. Digitization mandates and public access rights to non-confidential archives after twenty-five years.

STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS
Public records represent invaluable historical, administrative, and legal heritage. This bill creates a comprehensive statutory framework for archival preservation and digital governance under the State Archives Department.
