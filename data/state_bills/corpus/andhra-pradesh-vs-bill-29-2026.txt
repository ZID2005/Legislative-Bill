STATEMENT OF OBJECTS & REASONS
The Andhra Pradesh Panchayat Raj ACT 1994(ACT 13 of 1994) mandated
to elect one(1) President and one(1) Vice- President for every Mandal Praja
Parishad under section 153 from among the elected members of Mandal Praja
Parishad as specified in Clause-I of sub-section (1) of section 149.
This arrangement was in existence till the year 2021 until certain
amendments were carried out through enactment of “Act 14 of 2021”, in which
the office of second Vice- President was provided for each Mandal Praja
Parishad. Accordingly, section 153 and other relevant sections i.e. 148,156,165
and 170 of the APPR Act, 1994 were amended.
Similar amendments were carried out in respect of Zilla Praja Parishads
duly providing two Vice- chairpersons In place of one Vice- Chairperson, in the
same amendment Act i.e., “Act No.14 of 2021”. Accordingly, amendments were
carried out to sections i.e.181,187 &193 of the Act.
Amendments to the Act were also carried out in respect of section 250,
250-A and 260 of the Act to give common effect to both Vice- President of
Mandal Praja Parishad and Vice- Chairperson of Zilla Praja Parishad.
The arrangement for electing and positioning two (2) Vice-Presidents in
each Mandal Praja Parishad (MPP) and two (2) Vice-Chairpersons in each
Zilla Praja Parishad (ZPP) has been reviewed by the Government. It has been
observed that the intended objective of providing for a second Vice-President
in MPPs and a second Vice-Chairperson in ZPPs has not been achieved and
that the arrangement has not resulted in any improvement in the administration
of the respective MPPs and ZPPs.
The Government has, therefore, considered that continuation of the
second Vice-President and second Vice-Chairperson positions would not
serve any substantial administrative purpose and may be regarded as
superfluous to create plural leadership in the local governance and create
ambiguity without adding any value to improve administration. Hence, the
Government decided to withdraw the provision made for second Vice-President
position in MPP and second Vice- Chairperson position in ZPP.
The Government has decided to withdraw the provision for the second
Vice-President in MPPs and the second Vice-Chairperson in ZPPs, so as to
have a single Vice-President in each MPP and a single Vice-Chairperson in
each ZPP.
Accordingly, the Government has decided to restore the position
obtaining under the original Andhra Pradesh Panchayat Raj Act, 1994 (Act 13
of 1994), providing for one (1) Vice-President for every Mandal Praja Parishad

Panchayat Raj Act suitably.
The Bill seeks to give effect to the above decision.

MEMORANDUM REGARDING DELEGATED LEGISLATION
Clauses 1(2) of the Bill authorizes the Government to issue notifications
in respect of the matters specified therein and generally to carry out the
purposes of the Act.
All such notifications issued, which are intended to cover the matters
mostly of procedural in nature are to be laid on the table of the both Houses of
the State Legislature and will be subject to any modifications made by the
Legislature.
The above provisions of the Bill regarding delegated legislation are thus
of normal type and mainly intended to cover matters of procedure.
