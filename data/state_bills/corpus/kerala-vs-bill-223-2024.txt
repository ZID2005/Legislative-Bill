THE KERALA CLINICAL ESTABLISHMENTS (REGISTRATION AND REGULATION) AMENDMENT BILL, 2024
Bill No. 223 of 2024
A Bill to amend the Kerala Clinical Establishments (Registration and Regulation) Act, 2018.

Be it enacted by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Kerala as follows:-
1. Short title and commencement.- (1) This Act may be called the Kerala Clinical Establishments (Registration and Regulation) Amendment Act, 2024.
(2) It shall come into force on such date as the Government may notify.
2. Amendment of Section 14.- Mandatory digital renewal of licenses for hospitals, clinics, and diagnostic laboratories.
3. Enactment of standardized clinical audit protocols and patient charter display standards.

STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS
Quality assurance and transparency in public and private health facilities are critical for patient welfare. This amendment establishes online clinical registration portals and updates regulatory oversight under the Health & Family Welfare Department.
