THE TELANGANA PRIVATE UNIVERSITIES (ESTABLISHMENT AND REGULATION) (AMENDMENT) BILL, 2023
L.A. Bill No. 5 of 2023
A Bill further to amend the Telangana Private Universities (Establishment and Regulation) Act, 2018.

Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Telangana as follows:-
1. Short title.- This Act may be called the Telangana Private Universities (Establishment and Regulation) (Amendment) Act, 2023.
2. Inclusion of newly established multidisciplinary research institutions into the Schedule of recognized Private Universities.
3. Statutory student quota reservations and regulatory scrutiny over academic quality by the Higher Education Council.

STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS
To attract world-class academic institutions to Hyderabad and Telangana, expanding higher educational access while maintaining rigorous academic quality standards.
