THE INDIAN PARTNERSHIP (KERALA AMENDMENT) BILL, 2023
Bill No. 167 of 2023
A Bill further to amend the Indian Partnership Act, 1932 in its application to the State of Kerala.

Be it enacted by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Kerala as follows:-
1. Short title and commencement.- (1) This Act may be called the Indian Partnership (Kerala Amendment) Act, 2023.
2. Amendment of Schedule I.- Rationalization of statutory fees for registration of partnership firms, alteration of firm names, and inspection of registers.
3. Introduction of online registration through the State commercial portal.

STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS
The statutory fees prescribed under Schedule I of the Central Act of 1932 had become severely outdated. This amendment revises registration fee schedules and enables end-to-end electronic filing.
